1. Subnet for 4 offices using the network address fd00:2:: /88


Convert: 4 offices= 2 bits

Add:/88 +2 bits= /90  (6th butas, 3rd hex, 4i)

Ipasok sa butas si 4i, 4 times

fd00:0002:0000:0000:0000:0000:0000:0000 /90 1st office
                        :0040:2nd office
                        :0080:
                        :00C0:

2nd Office
Network:                  fd00:2::40:0:0/90
- 1st Valid (+1 Net):     fd00:2::40:0:1
- Last NonAny:            fd00:2::7f:ffff:ff7f
- 1st Any IP:             fd00:2::7f:ffff:ff80
- Last Any Ip (-1 Not):   fd00:2::7f:ffff:ffff
Not Yours:                fd00:2::80:0:0/90


2. Subnet for 52 offices using the network address fd00:3:0:33:: /112
   Determin the  6th subnet

Convert: 52 offices = 6 bits
Add: /112+6= /118  (8th butas, 2nd hex, 4i)
Ipasok: 4i sa butas 52 times but only until 6th subnet
  fd00:0003:0000:0033:0000:0000:0000:0000  /118
                                    :0400
                                    :0800
                                    :0c00
                                    :1000
                                    :1400-----6th subnet
                                    :1800

6th office:             fd00:3:0:33::1400 /118
-1st valid (+1 network): fd00:3:0:33::1401
-last non-any:           fd00:3:0:33::177f
-1st anycast IP:         fd00:3:0:33::1780     
-last anycast IP(-1 network) : fd00:3:0:33::17ff

-not yours:     fd00:3:0:33::1800 /118
